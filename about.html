About PetCare

PetCare is a college project that imagines a friendly place where shelters and adopters meet.

Our mission

Help every adoptable pet find a loving, permanent home by making adoption clear, kind and easy to understand.

Responsible adoption

A pet is a long-term commitment. We encourage adopters to:

  • Plan for food, vet care and time
  • Meet the pet before deciding
  • Never adopt on impulse

Welcome

Whether you want a playful puppy, a quiet senior cat or a gentle rabbit, you are in the right place.
