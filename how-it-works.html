How adoption works

Four simple steps from browsing to bringing your pet home.

🔎

1. Browse

Use search and filters on the Find a Pet page to meet pets that suit your home.

📝

2. Apply

Open a pet's page and choose Apply to Adopt. Tell the shelter a little about you.

💬

3. Talk and meet

The shelter reviews your application, answers questions and arranges a visit.

🏡

4. Take them home

Finish the paperwork, then welcome your new family member.
